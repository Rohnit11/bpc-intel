## Regulation
- **India import:** CDSCO, Drugs & Cosmetics Act 1940 + Cosmetics Rules 2020; Form COS-1 application -> COS-2 Registration Certificate via SUGAM portal; manufacturing site registration required; BIS IS 4707 ingredient standards (Part 2 revised Aug 2025); paper filings not accepted as of 2026
- **Korea export support:** MFDS regulatory-diplomacy MOUs to smooth exports
